FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How fast can I get up and running? ▼
Most teams are live within 5 minutes. Tell us your industry, and we auto-configure everything — modules, workflows, dashboards, the works. No coding, no consultants, no 6-month implementations.
Can I switch from Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho? ▼
Absolutely. We have one-click import tools for all major CRMs. Your contacts, deals, history, and custom fields come over cleanly. Most migrations take under an hour.
Is my data actually secure? ▼
SOC 2 Type II certified. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Multi-tenant isolation means other companies can never see your stuff. Regular penetration testing. 99.99% uptime track record.
What if my industry isn't listed? ▼
Pick Custom Industry during onboarding. Our AI analyzes your business and generates a tailored configuration from scratch. It learns your terminology, your processes, your KPIs.
Can I use it offline? ▼
The desktop apps (Mac, Windows, Linux) have full offline mode. Work without internet, and everything syncs automatically when you're back online.
Is there an API? ▼
Full REST and GraphQL API on Professional and Enterprise plans. Webhooks, real-time events, and SDKs for JavaScript, Python, Ruby, and PHP.
